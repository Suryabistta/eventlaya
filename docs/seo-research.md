# Eventlaya — SEO research: keywords, competitors, questions, content calendar

_Last updated: 2026-10-28_

Desk research for eventlaya.com's target searches in Nepal (English, Nepali and romanized Nepali), done with web search
and page fetches. Companion docs: `docs/seo.md` (what is built, owner checklist), `docs/seo-offsite-kit.md` (GBP,
socials, citations, outreach), `docs/seo-audit.md` (technical audit).

**Method and limits (read first)**
- The search tool used is US-based. It does **not** reproduce google.com.np results, the Google Maps "local pack", or
  Nepal-specific autocomplete. Competitor presence below means "found ranking/indexed for related queries", not
  "position N on Google in Nepal". The owner should re-check the top 10 queries from a phone in Nepal (incognito) and,
  after a few weeks, from Search Console.
- No keyword tool data was available: **there are no search volumes in this file**. Priority is a business judgement;
  difficulty is a **guess** from what the results looked like (who ranks, how strong, how specific).
- Every competitor fact links to the page it came from (section 9). Where something could not be confirmed it says
  "not verified".

## 1. Summary — top 10 findings

1. **No one publishes organizer-side guides for Eastern Nepal.** Searches for event management, venues and event
   companies in Itahari, Dharan and Biratnagar return Kathmandu firms, Indian/global directories, Facebook pages and
   hotel sites — no practical "how to run an event in <city>" content. Eventlaya's city guides have little direct
   competition (difficulty: low).
2. **"Best event management software (in Nepal)" is owned by global listicles** (Capterra, G2), which are not
   Nepal-specific (no NPR, no Khalti/eSewa, no offline gate, no Nepali). An honest, Nepal-specific comparison
   framework is a real gap.
3. **Ticket *buying* is crowded; ticket *selling* (organizer) content is thin.** Khalti (since 2019), TicketChha,
   EventMX, Khatra Tickets and Kgarira all sell tickets; only Kgarira publishes long-form buyer guides. Almost nothing
   explains, for organizers, how to choose between pay-at-venue, manual wallet transfer and integrated checkout.
4. **Khalti publishes its organizer tariff:** "standard tariff for the service charge is 20% of the total tickets sold
   via Khalti", tickets non-refundable, approval by Khalti's admin team. This is a factual comparison point (cite it,
   don't editorialise).
5. **Pageant / reality-show voting in Nepal = paid in-wallet voting** (Khalti for Miss Universe Nepal, Miss Nepal
   2022, Dancing Stars; eSewa for Nepal Idol) plus SMS short-code voting (Sparrow SMS, NTC). Khalti writes one
   "how to vote for <contest>" post per contest — a content type that wins these queries. No one writes about
   *running* a fair contest vote (OTP verification, vote caps, fraud flags, judges + public weighting).
6. **Regional pageants exist in Koshi** (Miss Itahari 2024, Miss Koshi 2024, Miss Purwanchal) and regional winners
   feed Miss Nepal — so "Miss <city> online voting" searches happen, but only when a contest is live. Target them with
   real client contest pages, not blog posts.
7. **Venue discovery for Koshi is handled by thin directories:** BiheBazaar lists party palaces in Itahari,
   Biratnagar and Birtamode (with some per-plate prices); Mero Najik has a Biratnagar city page with one listing;
   Pink Bazaar (an Indian site) lists 21 Biratnagar event planners. Dharan and Damak listings are sparse or missing.
   Eventlaya's marketplace + venue-choosing guides can compete, but local-pack results (Google Maps) will dominate
   "party palace <city>" — those need the venues' own Google profiles.
8. **Nepali-language phrasing is code-mixed.** Real usage seen: "इभेन्ट ब्यवस्थापन", "ईभेन्ट म्यानेजमेन्ट",
   "कार्यक्रम ब्यवस्थापन" (the Nepali event managers' association: कार्यक्रम ब्यवस्थापन संघ, नेपाल), Khalti's
   "Khalti मार्फत् Voting गर्नुहोस्" and "भोट". Nepali posts should mix English product words (QR, ticket, online,
   event) into natural Nepali, and include both व्यवस्थापन/ब्यवस्थापन spellings naturally where they fit.
9. **Permits content is written by law firms** (Niti Partners: ward recommendation → municipality → District
   Administration Office → police security plan), generic for all Nepal. A plain-language Koshi guide (with
   "check locally" honesty) would be useful and linkable.
10. **The local calendar is predictable and content-worthy:** Tihar (Laxmi Puja ≈ 8 Nov 2026, Bhai Tika ≈ 10 Nov),
    Chhath (≈ 15 Nov), Mangsir wedding season (≈ 17 Nov–15 Dec 2026), Udhauli (≈ 24 Dec 2026), Tamu Lhosar
    (≈ 30 Dec 2026), Maghe Sankranti (15 Jan 2027 = Magh 1, 2083), Birat Expo / Birat trade fair (Biratnagar, usually
    Dec–Jan but the 2083 edition opened in October 2026), Dharan Utsav (early January in 2025), Mechi/Ilam tourism
    festival (December in at least one year). Dates must be confirmed against the official calendar each year.
