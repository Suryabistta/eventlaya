To organize an event in Dharan, start with your audience: the city has an active music, youth and student scene, so concerts, band competitions, college fests and health-sector events are common and draw big, energetic crowds. Book a venue that suits your crowd size, inform the ward office and police for any public gathering, avoid open-air dates in the monsoon, and use QR passes with a proper gate plan so entry stays safe and fast. If your audience includes people from Dhankuta and the eastern hills, plan the timing around their travel.

## What kinds of events work well in Dharan?

Dharan is a sub-metropolitan city in Sunsari district at the foot of the hills, known for its music, youth and cultural life. Events that tap into that energy tend to do well.

Common event types:

- **Concerts and live music nights**, including local bands and touring acts.
- **Battle of the bands and talent shows**, where the audience wants a say in the winner.
- **College fests, sports meets and inter-college competitions.**
- **Health and medical events** such as conferences, workshops and student programmes, since Dharan is home to the B.P. Koirala Institute of Health Sciences (BPKIHS).
- **Cultural programmes** around festivals and community celebrations.

Each type needs a different setup. A seminar for doctors and a rock show might happen in the same week, but they share almost nothing except the need for a reliable guest list.

## How do you plan a concert or music event in Dharan?

Plan a concert around crowd safety and the gate first, then the stage. Music crowds in Dharan arrive in waves, often close to the start time, and the gate is where problems start.

1. **Set a hard capacity.** Agree on the number with the venue and stop selling at that limit.
2. **Use more than one gate.** A VIP or artist gate keeps guests and crew away from the main queue. An exit gate helps you count people leaving.
3. **Sell tickets in tiers.** Early Bird, General and VIP tickets with their own quantities help you measure demand early.
4. **Brief your volunteers.** Each gate staff member should know which ticket types their gate accepts.
5. **Plan for sound and power.** Confirm the generator, the sound system and the cut-off time with the venue and local authorities.
6. **Plan the exit as carefully as the entry.** Late-night crowds leaving together need lighting and marshals.

## How do you run audience voting at a band competition or talent show?

Run audience voting as a live poll that people open on their phones, and limit it to ticket holders so each pass gets one vote. Show the live results on a big screen to keep the crowd involved.

Tips for fair voting:

- Announce the voting rules on stage before the first performance.
- Open the poll only after all performers have played, or per round, so early acts are not disadvantaged.
- Keep the number of options short and use the performer names exactly as announced.
- Treat the audience vote as one part of the result if you also have a judging panel, and say so publicly.

## How do you organize a college fest or student event in Dharan?

Get the college's written approval first, then make registration and student verification simple. Student events often have many participants, several competitions and a tight budget.

- Build a registration form that asks for institution, year and which competitions the student is joining.
- Create a Student ticket type and a separate one for outside guests if prices differ.
- Use promo codes for partner colleges so you can see which campus registered the most.
- Assign a small crew to each competition and track tasks on a shared list.

## What should you know about health and academic events?

Health and academic events need accurate attendance records more than anything else. Organizers of conferences, workshops and training sessions often need to know exactly who attended which session.

- Collect full names exactly as they should appear on certificates.
- Ask for professional details only if you actually need them.
- Use check-in and check-out scans if attendance time matters for certificates.
- Export the attendee list as CSV after the event for records and certificates.

Also consider that many students and staff have fixed academic schedules. Check exam and duty periods before choosing a date.

## When is the best time to hold an event in Dharan?

The safest time for outdoor events is the dry season, outside the monsoon and the busiest festival weeks.

| Period | What to expect in Dharan | Planning tip |
|---|---|---|
| June–September | Heavy rain possible; hill roads can be disrupted | Go indoors or move the date |
| Late Sept–Nov | Dashain and Tihar; students and workers travel home | Expect smaller audiences and busy vendors |
| Mangsir (Nov–Dec) | Wedding season; banquets busy | Book venues early |
| Dec–Feb | Cool evenings, especially near the hills | Tell attendees to dress warmly for night events |
| Magh–Falgun (Jan–Mar) | Pleasant days, second wedding season | Good for outdoor events; book early |

Because Dharan is the gateway to Dhankuta and the eastern hills, some of your audience may travel down by road. Set start and end times that let them get home safely, and mention them clearly on the event page with both BS and AD dates.

## How do you keep entry smooth when mobile data is weak?

Prepare for slow mobile data whenever a large crowd gathers. Hundreds of phones in one place can make networks sluggish, right when your scanner needs them.

- Ask attendees to save or screenshot their QR pass before they leave home.
- Use a scanner with an offline queue for big events, so scans are stored and synced when the network returns.
- Keep a printed attendee list as a last-resort backup.
- Station one person at the gate just to help people find their pass.
