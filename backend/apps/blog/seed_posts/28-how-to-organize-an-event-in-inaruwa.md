To organize an event in Inaruwa, use its role as Sunsari's district headquarters: the District Administration Office and other district offices are in town, which suits official and district-level programmes and makes it easier to follow up on permissions in person. Plan for a multilingual audience, check festival dates including Chhath and Eid, book a venue with power backup and parking away from the East-West Highway, and register attendees or teams online before the day.

## What kinds of events suit Inaruwa?

Inaruwa is a municipality in Sunsari district on the Mahendra (East-West) Highway, and the district headquarters of Sunsari. That shapes the kind of events that work here.

- **District-level official programmes**: government and NGO meetings, trainings, cooperative assemblies and awareness campaigns that need people from across Sunsari.
- **Health camps and health programmes.** The government district hospital is in Inaruwa.
- **School and college events.** The municipality has dozens of schools and several colleges.
- **Sports tournaments.** Inaruwa has a track record here: night volleyball tournaments have been held in the town, and a national women's kabaddi competition was scheduled in Inaruwa from 26 Baisakh 2083 ([Kantipur](https://ekantipur.com/sports/2026/04/18/en/womens-national-kabaddi-final-week-in-inaruwa-winner-will-get-rs-100000-09-33.html)).
- **Community and religious programmes** for the town's Hindu, Muslim and other communities.
- **Nature and school trips** to Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve, for which Inaruwa is the nearest city.

Business-heavy events, such as big product launches, often go to [Itahari](/blog/how-to-organize-an-event-in-itahari) or [Biratnagar](/blog/how-to-organize-an-event-in-biratnagar) instead, so compare venues there if your audience is mostly from those cities.

## Who is your audience in Inaruwa?

Inaruwa's audience is multilingual and mixed in background, so a little extra effort in communication goes a long way. Maithili is the most common mother tongue, and Nepali, Urdu and Tharu are also widely spoken. Most residents are Hindu, with a sizeable Muslim community.

Practical tips:

- **Write the event page in clear Nepali**, and add the key details (date, time, venue, how to register) in Maithili if your audience is local.
- **Keep the registration form short.** Ask only what you need; long forms lose people who are less comfortable typing on a phone.
- **Put volunteers who speak Maithili at the gate and help desk.**
- **Respect religious calendars** when choosing dates and food, and offer vegetarian options.

People also come from villages around Inaruwa and along the highway, and some live near the roads that run south towards the Indian border. Add a "Which area are you coming from?" question to understand who is travelling. Guests flying in can use Biratnagar Airport, about 25 km from Inaruwa.

## When is the best time to hold an event in Inaruwa?

Check the festival calendar first, then the weather. Use this table as a starting point and confirm exact dates for your year.

| Period | What to expect | Tip |
|---|---|---|
| Falgun–Chaitra (Feb–Apr) | Holi; pleasant to warm weather | Good season for sports and outdoor events |
| Baisakh–Jestha (Apr–Jun) | Hot Terai afternoons | Prefer evening or night programmes with good lighting |
| Asar–Bhadra (Jun–Sep) | Monsoon rain and waterlogging in low areas | Avoid open grounds; check access roads |
| Asoj–Kartik (Sep–Nov) | Dashain, Tihar and Chhath | Chhath is a major Terai festival; avoid those days |
| Mangsir (Nov–Dec) | Main wedding season | Venues and caterers book early |
| Poush–Magh (Dec–Feb) | Cool days, dense morning fog is common | Start later; plan for slow road travel in fog |
| Any month | Eid (the date moves each year) | Check the year's date before announcing |

## How do you organize a sports tournament in Inaruwa?

Treat a tournament as two events: one for teams and one for spectators. Mixing them at a single gate creates confusion.

1. **Team registration.** Let each team manager register the whole team in one order, adding every player's name, so each player gets their own QR pass; set the maximum tickets per order to at least your largest squad size. Ask for the team name, manager's phone number and any age or eligibility details you check.
2. **Entry fees.** Collect them at the venue, or online with Khalti or eSewa where your ticketing tool supports it. Put the fee and the deadline on the event page.
3. **Player and spectator tickets.** Use a hidden Players ticket type unlocked with a promo code you send only to team managers, plus a free or paid Spectator type for the public.
4. **Separate gates.** Give players and officials their own entry, and allow each ticket type only at its gate.
5. **Night matches.** Check floodlights and the generator before the first match and keep spare fuel. Tell spectators the last match time so they can plan their way home.
6. **Capacity.** For covered halls, watch the live count of people inside and stop entry at a safe number.

## What kind of venue works in Inaruwa?

Choose by event type, then check power, parking and toilets.

- **Halls and banquets:** trainings, district meetings, receptions and award ceremonies.
- **School and college halls or grounds:** student events and community programmes, with written approval.
- **Covered halls and grounds:** sports tournaments, fairs and cultural programmes, outside the monsoon for outdoor use.
- **Hotel halls:** smaller meetings and trainings with guests from outside the district.

## Do you need permission for an event in Inaruwa?

Yes, for large public events. Start with your ward office and Inaruwa Municipality, and inform the local police. Because Sunsari's District Administration Office is in Inaruwa, it is practical to ask in person whether your event needs its approval, for example for a large public gathering, a night programme with loudspeakers, or an event on a public ground. Requirements depend on the event, so confirm them before you announce the date, and keep copies of letters at the gate.

## How do you plan a trip or event around Koshi Tappu?

Koshi Tappu Wildlife Reserve, near Inaruwa, is a protected area spread across Sunsari, Saptari and Udayapur districts, and together with the Koshi Barrage it is listed as an Important Bird Area. Wikipedia records 485 bird species there. That makes it popular for school trips, bird-watching outings and nature programmes.

- **Get permission** from the reserve authorities for any activity inside the protected area.
- **Register every participant** with an emergency contact and, for students, a guardian's phone number.
- **Plan the season.** Avoid the monsoon, when roads and low areas can flood.
- **Count people out and back.** Scan passes at the bus before leaving and again on return, so nobody is left behind.

## How do you promote an event in Inaruwa?

Use one registration link and trusted local channels.

- Share the event link on Facebook and TikTok and in WhatsApp and Viber groups.
- Ask schools, colleges, sports clubs, cooperatives and community organizations to share it.
- Give each partner a promo code so you can see who brought registrations.
- Put posters near the bus park, markets and schools with a QR code that opens the registration page.
