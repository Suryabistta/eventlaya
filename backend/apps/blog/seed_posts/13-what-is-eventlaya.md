Eventlaya (also written इभेन्टलय) is an event management web platform at eventlaya.com, built in Dharan for organizers in Eastern Nepal (Koshi Province). It lets clubs, colleges, schools, event companies and venues create an event page, take registrations and payments, send QR code passes, check people in at the gate with a phone, run audience polls and pageant or contest voting, and find vendors and venues through a marketplace.

## Who is Eventlaya for?

Eventlaya is for anyone organizing events in Eastern Nepal who wants registration, passes and check-in in one place. Its main users are:

- **Clubs and community groups** running programmes, concerts and meetups.
- **Colleges and schools** running fests, seminars, competitions and annual functions.
- **Event companies** managing events for their clients.
- **Venues and vendors** listing their services and availability in the marketplace.

Attendees do not need to install an app: they register on a web page and get their pass on screen and by email.

## What can you do with Eventlaya?

Eventlaya covers the attendee side of an event from the first announcement to the final headcount.

### How does the event lifecycle work?

Each event moves through draft, upcoming, live and ended (or cancelled), based on Nepal time. Registration stays open while the event is live, so walk-ins can register at the venue, and closes after the event ends.

### How does gate check-in work?

Gate staff log in with their own accounts and are assigned to gates. They scan passes with a phone camera; the scanner flags passes that are already used and shows gate notes such as "check student ID". Organizers see check-ins, check-outs and a live count of people inside the venue. More detail is in our guide to [QR code tickets and gate check-in](/blog/qr-code-tickets-gate-check-in-nepal).

## How do payments work on Eventlaya?

The organizer chooses, per event, how attendees pay: online with Khalti or eSewa, at the venue, or either, where online payments are enabled. Individual ticket types can be set to online only.

- **Online:** tickets are held for a few minutes while the attendee pays, and the pass is issued only after Eventlaya has confirmed the payment with Khalti or eSewa. Unpaid passes are refused at the gate.
- **At the venue:** the pass shows the amount due, and gate staff see what to collect when they scan it.
- **Money and refunds:** online payments are collected through Eventlaya's merchant accounts and paid out to the organizer; refunds are handled by hand and recorded on the organizer's Payments page.

## What is the Eventlaya marketplace?

The marketplace is a directory of event vendors and venues in Dharan, Itahari, Biratnagar and nearby. It includes photographers, sound and stage providers, decorators and caterers, as well as banquets and party palaces, resorts and lawns, auditoriums and conference halls, hotel ballrooms, open grounds and community centers.

Listings are reviewed before they go live. You can send an inquiry, or request one of the availability slots a venue or vendor has published; the owner then confirms or declines. Reviews come from real users. Browse it on the [marketplace page](/marketplace).
