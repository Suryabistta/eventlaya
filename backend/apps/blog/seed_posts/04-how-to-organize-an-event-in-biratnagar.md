To organize an event in Biratnagar, plan for a more formal, larger-scale audience than in most towns in the region: the city is Koshi Province's capital and a commercial and industrial hub, so conferences, trade expos, corporate meetings and institutional programmes are common. Book a venue with proper seating, sound and power backup early, coordinate with the ward office and police for public events, arrange travel for speakers who fly in, and use online registration and QR check-in so you can give sponsors and partners accurate numbers afterwards.

## What kinds of events are common in Biratnagar?

Biratnagar is a metropolitan city in Morang district and the capital of Koshi Province. Its role as a commercial, industrial and administrative centre shapes the kinds of events that happen there.

Typical events include:

- **Conferences and seminars** for industry, trade, education and the public sector.
- **Trade fairs and expos** where businesses display products to buyers and the public.
- **Corporate events** such as dealer meets, annual general meetings and staff training.
- **Award ceremonies and gala dinners** for associations and businesses.
- **Province-level programmes** that bring together people from across Koshi.
- **Cultural and community events**, including large festival gatherings.

Compared with a college fest, these events usually have more stakeholders: sponsors, partner organizations, invited guests and media. That means more paperwork, more seating plans and more people asking for numbers.

## How do you plan a conference in Biratnagar?

Plan a conference backwards from the date your speakers and sponsors need to confirm, and lock in the venue first. A typical timeline for a mid-sized conference looks like this.

| Time before event | Task |
|---|---|
| 10–12 weeks | Fix date and budget; shortlist and visit venues |
| 8–10 weeks | Book venue; invite keynote speakers; approach sponsors |
| 6–8 weeks | Publish event page with agenda; open registration |
| 4–6 weeks | Confirm speaker travel and hotel; order printing |
| 2–3 weeks | Send reminders; finalise seating and session halls |
| 1 week | Brief volunteers and gate staff; test the scanner |
| Event day | Check-in, session tracking, live updates |
| After | Export attendance; send sponsor reports and thank-you notes |

If your date falls in Mangsir or Magh–Falgun, add extra weeks: banquets and hotel halls are in demand for weddings then. A full [event planning checklist for Nepal](/blog/event-planning-checklist-nepal) can help you track the smaller tasks.

## How do you look after guests who fly in?

Treat out-of-town guests as a separate workstream with one person responsible. Biratnagar's domestic airport makes it easy for speakers and partners from Kathmandu and elsewhere to attend, but flights can be delayed or rescheduled.

- Collect each guest's flight details and phone number in advance.
- Assign one coordinator to airport pickups and drop-offs.
- Schedule keynote speakers away from the first session of the morning in case flights are late.
- Have a backup speaker or a flexible slot in the agenda.
- Share the venue's exact map location and a local contact number with every guest.

Some guests and vendors may also come through the Jogbani border crossing. If you invite participants from across the border, ask them to check travel requirements well ahead and give them clear arrival instructions.

## Which venues suit events in Biratnagar?

Pick a venue by the format of your event, not just its size. Biratnagar has hotel ballrooms, conference halls, banquets and open spaces, and each fits a different kind of programme.

- **Hotel ballrooms and conference halls:** good for seminars, conferences and gala dinners, especially when guests stay overnight.
- **Banquets and party palaces:** good for award nights and large seated programmes.
- **Open grounds and exhibition spaces:** suit trade fairs and expos, outside the monsoon and the hottest weeks.
- **Auditoriums:** suit talks and performances where the stage needs to be visible from every seat.

Visit before you book, and ask about power backup, air conditioning or fans, projector and sound, parking, and what time you must vacate. ## How do you plan around the Terai weather and festivals?

Plan around heat in April–May, rain from roughly June to September, and the festival season from late September to November. Biratnagar is in the Terai plains, so weather affects comfort as much as logistics.

- **Pre-monsoon heat:** prefer indoor, ventilated venues; keep water available; avoid long midday outdoor sessions.
- **Monsoon:** avoid open-ground expos; check that the venue's access roads do not flood.
- **Dashain and Tihar:** many people travel home, so audiences shrink and vendors are busy.
- **Chhath:** it is widely celebrated in the Terai, so check the date and avoid clashing with it.

Always show the Bikram Sambat date next to the AD date in invitations.

## How do you manage registration and entry at a large event?

Use online registration with ticket types for each audience group, and set up separate gates or scanning points for each entry. Large events in Biratnagar often have several audiences, such as delegates, exhibitors, VIP guests and the public, who should not share one queue.

1. **Create a ticket type per group.** For example: Delegate, Exhibitor, VIP, Student and Public. Hide types like VIP so only invited guests can see them through a promo code.
2. **Assign gates.** A delegate entrance, a VIP entrance and a public entrance reduce crowding.
3. **Restrict ticket types to gates.** VIP passes only work at the VIP gate, which stops crowding at the wrong entrance.
4. **Track check-in and check-out.** For multi-hall events, this shows how many people are in each space.
5. **Export the data.** Sponsors and partner organizations will ask for attendance, so export the attendee list as CSV.

## Do you need permission for a public event in Biratnagar?

For large public events, inform the ward office or the metropolitan city office and the local police in advance. What you need depends on the location, crowd size and type of event, so confirm locally.

Also check whether using a public ground, closing part of a road, putting up hoardings or playing amplified sound needs separate approval. Keep copies of letters on hand on the day.
